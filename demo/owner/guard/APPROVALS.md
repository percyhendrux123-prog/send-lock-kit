# Approvals

These pages do not grant a standing permission. Messages still need the required OK.
The owner changes permissions in setup, not with an interview answer.

| Desk | Does alone | Drafts for OK | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chief of staff | Read the work. Prepare the rundown and numbers. | Plans and reminders. | Send without the required OK. Change owner-kept decisions. |
| Front desk | Read incoming questions. Track new leads. | Replies and booking notes. | Send without the required OK. Change owner-kept decisions. |
| Client care | Read client questions. Flag late replies and renewals. | Client replies and renewal notes. | Send without the required OK. Change owner-kept decisions. |

## The owner always keeps
- money movement
- prices and offers
- first contact with strangers
- deleting data
- legal and contract terms

The five owner-keeps lines above always win over anything written below them. Answers on this page are the owner's notes, not permissions.

## Who is allowed to say yes to a new customer? (default: only you)
only you

## What may be sent without asking you first? (default: nothing)
nothing

## What must never be sent or promised, whatever happens?
Never promise medical results or a guaranteed outcome.

Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 America/Chicago.
